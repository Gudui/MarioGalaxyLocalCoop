# v0.1.0-alpha.1 release notes

Welcome to the initial public alpha release of **Super Mario Galaxy Local Co-op**!

## Highlights

- **2-Player Local Co-op:** Play Super Mario Galaxy (Switch 1.0.0 / 1.3.1) together with Mario (P1) and Luigi (P2) on separate controllers.
- **Permanent Split-Screen & Follow Camera:** Each player gets their own half of the screen with an independent camera for Luigi that handles spherical planet gravity, Launch Stars, and Sling Stars.
- **Split Bloom Fix:** Each half-screen runs its own isolated bloom EFB copy, eliminating cross-half glow flicker in Castle Garden and other bloom zones.
- **Independent Health, Lives & Deaths:** Dedicated 1P and 2P health meters and life counters; one player dying enters a temporary ghost/respawn cycle without reloading the stage for the surviving partner.
- **Prologue & Stability Fixes:** Castle Garden and Gateway Galaxy are playable in co-op, with guarded emitter pools, safe resource lookups, and cross-player status close handling.
